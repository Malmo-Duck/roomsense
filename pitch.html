Why every hotel runs one flat setback

  • Empty-room heating: a top opex + carbon line
  • Sun, floor, shading, bookings: all knowable, all ignored
  • The real blocker is fear: one guest, one cold room
  • So setbacks stay shallow. Savings stay on the table

Savings is the output. The product is the promise: never miss check-in comfort. Recovery time is predicted per room, before the setback is applied.

⚠ Uncertainty policy
  • Every number traced: signal, confidence, weight
  • Signal down → system says so, keeps working
  • Low confidence → shallower setback. Doubt favors the guest
